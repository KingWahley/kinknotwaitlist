Frequently Asked Questions

How does vendor verification work?
We perform multi-step background checks, including identity verification, business registration audits, and mandatory review cycles from past couples to ensure every vendor is professional and reliable.
What is escrow payment protection?
Your payments are held securely in a protected account and only released to the vendor after they fulfill their service, protecting you from no-shows and fraud.
When will the full platform launch?
We are currently in a private beta phase. By joining the waitlist, you'll be among the first to receive an invite as we roll out in late 2024.
How can I join as a vendor?
Simply select "Wedding Vendor" in the waitlist form. We'll prioritize early-onboarding for vendors who join the waitlist now.
Is KinKnot available in my city?
We are launching initially in major urban hubs across Nigeria, including Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt, with plans to expand rapidly in 2025.
